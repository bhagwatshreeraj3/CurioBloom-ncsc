Scientific principles & step-by-step experiment guide

🔬 Real Chemical Formula / Scientific Law:

NaHCO₃ + C₆H₈O₇ ➔ CO₂ + H₂O + Na₃C₆H₅O₇

📦 Materials Included In Kit:

  • Citric Acid Crystals (Non-Toxic)
  • Sodium Bicarbonate Powder
  • Measuring Flask & Beaker

🧪 Step-by-Step Procedure:

  1. Measure 20ml of distilled water into the beaker.
  2. Add 2 scoops of Baking Soda and stir until dissolved.
  3. Pour 1 scoop of Citric Acid and observe immediate CO2 bubbling!
